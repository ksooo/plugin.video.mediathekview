v1.3.0
- Posters and episode pictures from themoviedb.org, with genre and age rating. Off until you enter a token from your own free TMDB account.
- Duplicate "Audiodeskription" and "Gebärdensprache" versions can be hidden. Both off by default.
- Film lists show the watched state and a film's picture beside the list, as Kodi does with episodes.

v1.2.0
- The add-on is now called MediathekView+. Only the name changed; your settings and your database stay where they are.
- Kodi stays usable while the film database is updated.
- Shows with several seasons open on their seasons instead of one long list, and their episodes are in episode order.
- Films say what they are: season and episode as "1x11.", the broadcast date as a date, whether they have subtitles, which channel they are from, and whether the stream is HD. All of that used to be packed into the title and the description.
- Kodi keeps the view you picked for a folder. The add-on no longer chooses one for you.
- tagesschau24, ZDFinfo and ZDFneo show their logo again.

v1.1.0
- Updating the film database no longer gets in the way. The downloaded file is unpacked several times faster, the progress is shown, and it can be interrupted.
- Searching is behind one menu entry instead of two. "Quick Search" and "Search" were the same search with a different number of fields.
- Saved searches have been removed. Searches saved with an earlier version are no longer offered.
- The search history keeps the last 25 terms instead of growing for as long as the add-on is used.

v1.0.1
- Fixed: after a fresh installation the add-on did not start at all. Neither the user interface nor the background update would come up.
- An unreadable setting no longer stops the add-on. It falls back to the intended default and notes that in the log.

v1.0.0
- First release of this fork.
